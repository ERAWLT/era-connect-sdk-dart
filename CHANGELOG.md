## 0.1.0

Initial release: feature parity with `@hwlt/era-connect` 0.5.x (the TypeScript
SDK). Account linking and air-gapped UR/QR transaction signing for EVM,
Bitcoin (+ Litecoin, Dogecoin, Dash, Bitcoin Cash), Solana, Tron, TON,
Cardano, Sui, Cosmos and XRP, plus the `verify` layer proving the device
signed exactly what was sent.
